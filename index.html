Dead Pixel vs. Stuck Pixel: What is the Difference?

A dead pixel is a pixel where all sub-pixels remain permanently unpowered, appearing as a persistent black dot on bright backgrounds. A stuck pixel occurs when one or more sub-pixels (red, green, or blue) remain locked in an "on" state, showing a brightly colored dot on darker scenes.

Recommended Testing Steps

  • Clean the display with a dry microfiber cloth before testing to eliminate dust particles.
  • Click any solid color above to enter true full-screen testing.
  • Use the Left and Right Arrow keys or click anywhere on the screen to switch between test colors.
  • Examine the display on pure white and solid primary colors to spot dead sub-pixels, and test pure black in a darkened room to assess backlight bleed and panel uniformity.
  • Press Esc at any time to return.

Can Stuck Pixels Be Fixed?

While physically dead pixels are permanent hardware defects, stuck sub-pixels can sometimes be revived by cycling colors rapidly or applying gentle pressure around the affected spot with a microfiber cloth.
