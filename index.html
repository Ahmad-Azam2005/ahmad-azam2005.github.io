Hardware Knowledge Base & Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guides for diagnosing panel defects, OLED degradation, and sensor responsiveness.

How do you test for OLED burn-in on monitors, laptops, and phones?
OLED panels use organic sub-pixels that age at differing rates. Inspecting pure black, pure white, stepped grays (5%, 25%, 50%), and pure magenta reveals static taskbars, mobile notches, and UI elements. If faint ghost silhouettes remain on these uniform fields, permanent image retention has occurred.
How do I fix a stuck pixel on a monitor or laptop display?
Run our ⚡ Stuck Pixel Reviver for 15 to 30 minutes. Rapid 22Hz transitions between primary RGB values stimulate liquid crystal molecules to unlock and return to standard orientation.
What is the difference between IPS glow and backlight bleed?
• Backlight Bleed: A physical assembly flaw where bezel pressure allows light to escape from display edges; it stays fixed at all angles.
• IPS Glow: An optical byproduct of IPS crystal structure that shifts position, color tint, and intensity as you move off-axis.
