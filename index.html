Monitor Test Setup Guide

Follow these steps to obtain accurate evaluation results:

  • Clean your display surface with a microfiber cloth to remove dust particles that can mimic dead pixels.
  • Disable ambient lighting or dim the room to properly detect backlight bleed during uniformity tests.
  • Set your monitor to its native resolution at 100% display scaling in your operating system.
  • Use Spacebar, Arrow Keys, or click anywhere on the screen to navigate through test screens.
