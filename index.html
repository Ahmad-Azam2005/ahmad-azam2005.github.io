Display Defect Metrology (ISO 9241-307)

Modern liquid crystal displays (IPS, VA, TN) and Organic Light Emitting Diode (OLED) panels comprise millions of individual sub-pixels. Mechanical or electrical flaws manifest in distinct failure modes:

Defect Category Visual Presentation Physical Mechanism ISO 9241 Class II Policy
Dead Pixel Permanent dark speck across light fields Thin-film transistor (TFT) unpowered or open-circuit Up to 2 per million tolerated
Stuck Pixel Continuous illuminated color (Red, Green, Blue) Liquid crystal physically locked in state Up to 5 per million tolerated
Backlight Bleed Cloudy white/amber glow along display edges Mechanical frame pressure pinching panel layers Evaluated via darkroom uniformity test

Stuck Pixel Reviver & Rapid Flasher

High-frequency sub-pixel exercise utility. Rapidly forces liquid crystals to switch between primary RGB states to stimulate sluggish or stuck sub-pixels.

⚠️ Photosensitive warning: Contains rapid screen flashes.
Recommended Procedure: Run the flasher for 15 to 30 minutes. If the sub-pixel remains stuck, power down the screen and apply gentle pressure using a clean microfiber cloth around the coordinate while re-powering.

About DisplayCore

DisplayCore is an open web metrology utility engineered to verify display panel health, digitizer responsiveness, and acoustic transducer performance for desktop monitors, laptops, and mobile phones.
